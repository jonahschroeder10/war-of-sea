How to Play

  • Objective: Sink all enemy ships before your own fleet is destroyed
  • Your Grid: The left board shows your ships. Guard them well!
  • Enemy Grid: The right board is where you attack the enemy
  • Taking Turns: Click on enemy grid cells to attack
  • Hits & Misses: Red indicates a hit, light blue indicates a miss
  • Strategy: Remember where you've attacked and learn from your hits
  • Victory: Eliminate all enemy ships to win the game!
